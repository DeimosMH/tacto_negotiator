How to read this dashboard

Start with the score, then check validity. Scores compare total cost against the evaluator’s reference. A low price alone does not make a package good: payment, freight, tooling and constraints also matter. Null costs mean unknown or unavailable, never zero.

Follow a match from left to right. Filter a run, select a case, then pick a step in its timeline. “Why” translates the recorded controller reason. It is not a model’s private reasoning. Proposed and submitted actions differ when the runner overrides a late decision.

Keep timing and model use separate. Elapsed time is the simulated negotiation clock, including supplier delay; decision time is measured locally. Model attempts can include unavailable callbacks or validation failures. They are not a billable API-call count. Wave labels are repeated trials, not a shared live leaderboard.

Saved evidence only. This dashboard opens competition reports and cannot start runs or contact the arena. Refresh reloads saved results; in-progress checkpoints without matches are omitted. JSON opened from your computer stays in your browser.
